The core question

How is digitalization changing competition and the distribution of value across Nepal’s payments ecosystem?

Last updated: 21 August 2026. Data cut-off: July 2025 unless noted.

What changed

Three signals of a structural shift

3×

Reported payment activity

All NRB-reported channels rose from 0.57bn transactions in 2021 to 1.71bn in 2024.

210×

QR monthly scale

QR count increased from 195k in July 2020 to 40.9m in July 2025.

2.26

Mobile transactions per user

Monthly mobile-banking engagement rose from 0.52 to 2.26 transactions per user.

All-channel activity is reported-channel activity—not unique payments. It includes ATM cash, ECC and RTGS; the retail series below excludes them.

The evidence

Growth moved from access to everyday use

Retail digital activity grew faster than the headline total

Digital retail transactions rose from 0.46bn in 2021 to 1.56bn in 2024—a 50.0% CAGR. The composition of activity matters as much as the aggregate total.

Use the chart as a directional system-level view; it cannot allocate activity to individual banks, wallets, or PSPs.

Who does what

Payments is a layered market

01

Regulation and settlement

Nepal Rastra Bank sets rules, oversees payment institutions, and operates RTGS.

02

PSO infrastructure

Clearing, switching, card, QR, and interbank rails—such as NCHL, Fonepay and SCT—move payment instructions.

03

PSP and bank distribution

Wallets and banks own the customer and merchant interfaces, but NRB’s aggregate data does not disclose their individual market shares.
