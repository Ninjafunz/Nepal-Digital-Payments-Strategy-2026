# Research Question Document

**Project:** From Cash to Platforms: The Transformation of Nepal's Digital Payments Industry, 2021–2026

---

## Core Research Question

> **How is digitalization changing the basis of competition and the distribution of economic value in Nepal's payments ecosystem?**

This question has two embedded components:

1. **Basis of competition** — What are the strategic variables that determine who wins? How have they shifted as Nepal moves from cash to digital?
2. **Distribution of economic value** — Who captures value in the payments ecosystem? How is that shifting across banks, wallets, PSPs, infrastructure providers, and regulators?

---

## Scope Boundaries

### Geographic Scope
- **Primary:** Nepal (all seven provinces)
- **Focus markets:** Kathmandu Valley (highest digital adoption), with national-level data
- **Exclusion:** Cross-border payments (unless directly relevant to domestic competitive dynamics)

### Temporal Scope
- **Primary window:** FY 2020/21 (mid-2020) through FY 2025/26 (mid-2026)
- **Historical context:** Pre-2021 data used for trend context only
- **Forward-looking:** Scenarios for 2027–2030 (clearly marked as projection, not data)

### Industry Scope
- **In scope:** Digital payment channels, payment service providers, payment system operators, banks offering digital payments, mobile money, wallets, QR payments, card payments, internet banking, mobile banking, connectIPS, POS, ATMs, merchant ecosystem
- **Out of scope:** Cryptocurrency, blockchain-based payments (not material in Nepal), lending products, insurance, wealth management (except where they create strategic advantage in payments)

### Institutional Scope
- Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) — regulator and primary data source
- Licensed banks and financial institutions (BFIs) offering digital payments
- Licensed Payment Service Providers (PSPs)
- Licensed Payment System Operators (PSOs)
- Mobile network operators (as payment channels, not telecom analysis)

## Key Term Definitions

These definitions are binding for the entire project. If a term is used, it means what it says here.

| Term | Definition | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **Digital payment** | Any payment transaction initiated or completed through an electronic channel | Includes mobile banking, internet banking, wallets, QR, cards, POS, connectIPS |
| **Payment channel** | A specific method of executing digital payments (e.g., mobile banking, wallet, QR, POS, card) | Distinguished by the technology and interface, not the institution |
| **PSP (Payment Service Provider)** | An entity licensed by NRB to provide payment services | e.g., eSewa, Khalti |
| **PSO (Payment System Operator)** | An entity that operates payment infrastructure | e.g., Nepal Clearing House Limited (NCHL) |
| **Wallet** | A stored-value account operated by a PSP, allowing users to make payments without a bank account | Also called mobile wallet or digital wallet |
| **Mobile banking** | Banking services accessed via mobile phone, linked to a bank account | Distinct from wallets — requires a bank account |
| **Interoperability** | The ability for users of different payment systems to transact with each other | e.g., connectIPS enables cross-platform payments |
| **Network effects** | A phenomenon where the value of a service increases as more users adopt it | Includes same-side (more users = more valuable) and cross-side (more users attract more merchants) |
| **Customer ownership** | Control over the primary customer relationship and data in a payment transaction | Who "owns" the customer in the value chain |
| **Profit pool** | The total profits available in an industry, distributed across segments | Adapted from PvP (Gadiesh & Gilbert) |
| **Strategic group** | A group of firms in an industry following similar strategies along key dimensions | Identified through data, not assumed |
| **Value chain** | The full range of activities required to complete a payment transaction | From initiation to settlement, including all intermediaries |
| **Stock variable** | A measure at a point in time (e.g., total registered users) | Distinguished from flow variables |
| **Flow variable** | A measure over a period (e.g., monthly transactions) | Distinguished from stock variables |
| **HHI (Herfindahl-Hirschman Index)** | A measure of market concentration: HHI = Σ(si²) | Used to assess competitive concentration |
| **NPR** | Nepalese Rupee | All monetary values in this project are in NPR unless stated otherwise |

## What This Project Is NOT

To prevent scope creep and maintain analytical rigor:

1. **Not a history of digital payments in Nepal.** We study a five-year window with strategic intent.
2. **Not a technology review.** We study competitive dynamics, not blockchain or fintech trends.
3. **Not a financial inclusion study.** Inclusion is relevant where it affects competitive dynamics.
4. **Not a regulatory policy paper.** We study regulation's competitive impact, not prescribe policy.
5. **Not an investment recommendation.** We produce strategic analysis, not stock picks.
